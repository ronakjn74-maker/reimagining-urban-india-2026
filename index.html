A different kind of gathering

This convention is different.

We are not merely discussing cities — we are reimagining them.

India is experiencing unprecedented urban transformation. The challenge is no longer simply to build more, but to build better: creating cities that are sustainable, resilient, inclusive, healthy and truly livable.

The National Convention on “Reimagining Urban India: Pathways to Livable Cities” creates a platform for knowledge exchange, policy discussion, technical deliberation and collaboration among the professionals shaping India’s urban future.
