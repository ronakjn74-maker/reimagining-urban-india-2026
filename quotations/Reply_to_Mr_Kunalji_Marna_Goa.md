Subject: Re: Structural Consultancy Proposal – Marna Project, Goa – Clarifications

Dear Kunal ji,

Thank you for your detailed note on our proposal for the Marna project, Goa. Please find our point-wise clarifications below. The commercial terms in our quotation dated 30 October 2026 remain as quoted; the clarifications on scope and working arrangements are set out here and will be incorporated into the revised proposal.

1. Fee and chargeable area
Our fee of INR 15 per sq. ft. is based on the total built-up area of the project, with a minimum fee of INR 5,00,000 per building, plus GST at 18%. These rates are already our best offer for the complete agreed scope and we are unable to revise them.
Chargeable area is the total built-up area of all levels, counted at 100%: basement, stilt, ground and all upper floors, together with balconies and terraces. No separate area is counted or charged for pools, water tanks, STP structures or other service structures; these are covered within the structural scope described in point 4.

2. Revisions
Unlimited revisions within the agreed project scope are included until structural completion. This covers revisions arising from architectural development, MEP coordination, authority comments and site requirements, and any corrections to our own design or drawings, at no additional charge.
Should the overall design concept change repeatedly after the structural scheme has been frozen, the way forward will be discussed and agreed mutually before further work proceeds. Any major change outside the agreed scope will be quoted separately and taken up only after your written approval.

3. Site visits and inspections
Site visits to Goa will be made as and when required by the progress of work, including critical stages such as foundations and reinforcement checks before concreting. A written inspection report will be issued after each visit.
Visits at critical stages will be attended by our senior engineers; routine visits will be attended by engineers from our team. Travel from Mumbai, accommodation and local transport for all visits are to be arranged and borne by the client. Urgent site queries will be attended to over phone or video call, with a visit arranged where the situation requires it.

4. Structural scope
All structural elements of the project, from foundation to the topmost level, are covered within our scope. This includes foundations, basement and stilt structures, retaining walls, ramps, staircases, lift pits, balconies, private and rooftop pools, the common infinity pool, jacuzzis, water tanks, STP structures and terrace MS framing and pergolas.
The excavation and shoring scheme is normally proposed by the geotechnical consultant. We will work with the geotechnical consultant and assist in finalising the scheme.

5. Drawings and deliverables
We will issue Valid for Construction (VFC) drawings for site in both CAD and PDF format, duly signed and stamped, including reinforcement details. Design calculations will be furnished where required, for example for authority submission or specific elements on request.
Structural BOQ and quantity estimates are not within our scope. Preparation or checking of bar bending schedules, review of contractor shop drawings and review of as-built drawings are likewise not included; these can be taken up separately if required.

6. Coordination and design optimisation
We will coordinate with your architect, MEP, pool and other specialist consultants on service openings, equipment loads, terrace landscaping and related interfaces. Our design approach aims at an efficient structure that optimises steel and concrete quantities while fully meeting code requirements for safety, durability and the architectural intent.

7. Soil investigation and approvals
Soil investigation is to be arranged and paid for by the client through a geotechnical agency. We will specify the investigation requirements and review the report for design.
Structural submissions, certificates and completion documents required for Goa approvals will be provided by us, unless otherwise stated. Any statutory fees, or charges of a third-party proof checker if one is mandated, would be to the client's account.

8. Timelines and support
Our normal turnaround is 3 to 4 working days for each design stage and for revisions, from receipt of complete inputs. VFC drawings are released one stage ahead of site: for example, when foundation work commences on site, the VFC drawings for the stilt level will be released, and so on through the building. A stage-wise schedule will be shared on receipt of the final architectural drawings and the site programme.

We trust this clarifies all the points raised. We will issue the revised proposal incorporating the above, and look forward to working with you on the Marna project.
